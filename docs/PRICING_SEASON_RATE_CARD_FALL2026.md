# PFLX Season Rate Card — Fall Season 2026

Source: `PFLX_Season_Rate_Card.pdf` (Ennis, uploaded Sept 5, 2026), transcribed in full below
so the pricing/programme structure lives in the project's own docs rather than only as a PDF
attachment in chat. This is a reference document — nothing in the app reads or enforces these

treat this file as the source of truth to implement against, not the other way around.

**Prototype FLX · Digital Apprenticeship Programme · Ages 11–18**
Fall Season 2026 · 10 weeks · Late Aug – Late Oct · USD · AED · Rev 1.0 — 22 Aug 2026
AED at the pegged rate 3.6725 · Valid through the Fall 2026 season · thetomorrowteacher.org

Students join a fictional startup studio and spend ten weeks doing the work — designing games,
building worlds, producing media, pitching ventures. Everything below is priced **per season**.
There is no monthly commitment, no per-teacher licence, and **no charge at all for the apps** —
those are free to every student, permanently. What is priced here is the programme around them.

## 01 · Player — the free tier

The tools are free. All of them, permanently, to anyone — this is not a trial that expires. A
whole class can work here indefinitely without paying anything.

| Tier | Price (USD) | Price (AED) |
|---|---|---|
| Player — unlimited, no expiry | Free | Free |

Includes: every PFLX app, account, avatar and studio brand, plus open Programs, open Projects,
and every Node across the Core Pathways (Game Designer, Content Creator, 3D Modeler, Digital
Artist, and the Professional Entrepreneur minor). A limited X-Coin allowance is earned from that
open content.

What a Player does **not** get: the season structure — no Programs beyond the open ones, no
specialised Projects, no Checkpoints, and so no Signature Badges tied to gated Programs or
Modules, no Jobs, no Exclusive Badges, and no season credential.

## 02 · Season Pass — individual student

One student, one season, bought by the family. You are not paying for the software (that's free
above, and stays free) — you are paying for the structure: the Programs, the specialised
Projects, the Checkpoints, and the rank and portfolio a student earns by getting through them.
It ends when the season ends; nothing renews on the card.

Season structure: 10-week season · 5 Checkpoints (Alpha–Omega) · 4 startup studios · 5 career
pathways.

| Tier | Price (USD) | Price (AED) |
|---|---|---|
| Season Pass (1 student) | $149 | 550 |
| Season Pass — Early Bird (closes 4 weeks out) | $119 | 425 |
| Sibling Pass (2nd child +, any season) | $109 | 400 |
| Annual Player Pass (Fall + Winter + Spring + Summer Expansion — 4 seasons for the price of 3, billed once) | $447 | 1,650 |

A Season Pass unlocks the full season: every Program and specialised Project, placement in one
of the four startup studios, all five Checkpoints from Alpha through Omega, full X-Coin and XP
earning, submissions with host feedback, system events, unlockables, and an end-of-season
portfolio artifact and rank certificate. Every Signature Badge tied to the Programs, Projects and
Modules a pass unlocks is in reach. Jobs and their Exclusive Badges are a Cohort feature (see
Level 3).

## 03 · Cohort — one school, one programme

One school, one programme, one named cohort. Host seats, setup and onboarding are included —
you are not buying software and then paying again to be shown how it works. A cohort is also the
only way a student reaches Jobs, where the rarest badges in PFLX are earned.

| Tier | Price (USD) | Price (AED) |
|---|---|---|
| Cohort 15 (15 seats + 2 hosts) | $1,490 | 5,475 |
| Cohort 30 (30 seats + 3 hosts) — the standard deal | $2,490 | 9,150 |
| Cohort 60 (60 seats + 5 hosts) | $3,990 | 14,650 |
| Additional seat (per student, past a band's cap) | $65 | 250 |

- **Cohort 15**: named cohort space, studio and branding, Mission Control project setup, season
  calendar, dedicated channel, host onboarding call, host dashboard. Where the cohort is attached
  to a Program or Project that creates Jobs, students can take them on and earn Exclusive
  Badges — the tier of badge that exists nowhere else in PFLX.
- **Cohort 30**: everything in Cohort 15, plus a cohort leaderboard, Friday Showdown hosting, and
  a mid-season progress review. This is the standard deal and the one most schools should be
  looking at.
- **Cohort 60**: everything in Cohort 30, plus two named sub-studios inside the cohort and
  priority support for the length of the season.
- **Additional seat**: any seat past a band's cap — cheaper than jumping a band for the sake of a
  handful of students.

## 04 · Organisation — multiple cohorts, one shared economy

For a school running several cohorts at once — a media club, a robotics team, a business
society, a year group. Each gets its own studio, brand and mission set, and they compete against
each other inside one shared X-Coin economy.

| Tier | Price (USD) | Price (AED) |
|---|---|---|
| Org Essential (3 cohorts · 90 seats) — 16% below three separate cohorts | $6,300 | 23,125 |
| Org Plus (6 cohorts · 180 seats) — 24% below buying separately | $11,400 | 41,875 |
| Org Campus (12 cohorts · 360 seats) — 34% below buying separately | $19,800 | 72,725 |
| Org Enterprise (360+ seats, multi-campus) | Quote | Quote |

- **Org Essential**: org admin dashboard, cross-cohort leaderboard, inter-cohort Friday Showdown,
  shared economy.
- **Org Plus**: everything above, plus season theming, host certification for up to six staff,
  and a termly impact report.
- **Org Campus**: everything above, plus a co-designed season storyline, sponsor integration, and
  a named point of contact.
- **Org Enterprise**: districts, networks and multi-campus groups — quoted per engagement.

## Choosing — four questions, four answers

- **"We want to try it before we budget for it."** Put the class on Player and leave them there.
  It costs nothing, it does not expire, and the students will tell you within a fortnight whether
  it lands.
- **"A few interested students, no school budget line."** Families buy Season Passes directly —
  AED 550 for a ten-week term, roughly what a term of swimming costs, and no admin lands on your
  side at all.
- **"One club or elective, one teacher running it."** Cohort 30 — AED 9,150 for ten weeks works
  out at AED 305 per student, and host onboarding is included.
- **"Several student groups, and we want them competing."** Org Essential upward. The
  cross-cohort leaderboard and shared economy are the whole point — separate cohorts cannot do
  this.

## On the economy, plainly

X-Coins are earned, never bought. There is no way to convert money into currency, rank or
badges — every coin comes from work submitted and reviewed, and free Players earn them too, at a
limited rate, from the open content. What a paid tier buys is access to more of the work: the
Programs, specialised Projects and Checkpoints where the bulk of the economy lives, the Signature
Badges tied to completing them, and — at Cohort level — the Jobs that carry Exclusive Badges. So
a paying student can go further than a free one. They still cannot buy a single coin, a rank, or
a badge outright. Every one is worked for.
